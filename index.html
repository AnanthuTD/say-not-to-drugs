The Path of Addiction

1 Month

Initial dependency forms. Cravings begin to affect daily decisions and priorities.

3 Months

Financial strain begins. Money previously used for necessities now diverted to substances.

6 Months

Relationships become strained. Trust is broken with family and close friends.

1 Year

Significant health decline. Physical and mental health issues become apparent.

2+ Years

Complete life disruption. Career, housing, and long-term health severely compromised.
